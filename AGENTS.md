# CodeIntel project guidance

Read [README.md](README.md#maintained-project-documentation) before development or
review. It indexes the maintained workflows, requirements and design decisions
in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md), [CONTRACTS.md](CONTRACTS.md) and the relevant [usage guide](docs/USAGE.md).
Update affected documents in the same change; independent review checks their
consistency with the exact change and preserves unimplemented intent and dated
source/runtime evidence. Issues track execution and link these documents.

Resolve the selected source, runtime and existing index before acting. Confirm
source/AST locators and coverage; a missing graph edge is not proof of absent
behavior. Preserve unrelated edits, recovered branches, draft designs and original
indexes/receipts. Follow the active task's ownership and execution boundaries;
these instructions do not grant indexing, provider, credential or service access.

Document discovery is distinct from complete loader delivery or instruction
adherence. A linked document is not implicitly injected; read the relevant text.
